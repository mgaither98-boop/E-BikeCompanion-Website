Become an E-Bike Companion tester

We're looking for Android users and e-bike owners to help test the app, find bugs and improve bike compatibility information before the first public release.

Testing recruitment is open. Google Play access is invitation-only while we prepare the test track. Submit a private application below; accepted testers will receive the approved opt-in link by email once it is available.
📱

General Android testing

Check startup, navigation, settings, ride screens, app stability and offline behaviour across different Android phones.

🚲

E-bike compatibility

If you own an e-bike, help document the model, display and controller where known, and report what the app can actually observe.

🗺️

Maps and ride tools

Try importing supported MBTiles maps and GPX routes, and test ride recording while stationary or in a safe, controlled situation.

🔬

Read-only research

Help evaluate Bluetooth discovery and diagnostic clarity. Experimental drivers are intended to remain read-only; don't test while riding.

Apply to become a tester

Use this private application form to tell us what you can test. Applications will be sent to the E-Bike Companion tester system when it is connected. You will not be asked to publish your email address or application in GitHub.

Applications are reviewed manually. Submitting this form does not automatically grant access. If accepted, you will receive an invitation by email with the Google Play testing instructions when the test track is ready.
What can you test?

Please do not submit GPS routes, serial numbers, credentials, health data or other sensitive information. See our privacy policy. The form will only submit after the private application endpoint is configured.

Who we're looking for

  • Android users willing to install a test build and try a short checklist.
  • E-bike owners, especially people willing to share their bike model and hardware details.
  • Testers who can describe problems clearly and retest fixes when asked.

How to get involved

  1. Read the privacy policy and testing and safety guidance.
  2. Submit the private application form and wait for manual review.
  3. If accepted, follow the Google Play opt-in link and installation instructions sent to your email.
  4. Use the public community repository for bug reports and compatibility evidence only; never post your email address there.

Visit public community ↗ Contribution guide

Privacy and safety come first

  • Do not share GPS routes, home/work locations, serial numbers, credentials, tokens or unredacted screenshots in public reports.
  • Inspect any diagnostic export before sharing. Reports should contain only information needed to investigate the issue.
  • Participation is voluntary. Don't perform research or troubleshoot while riding, and never rely on experimental features for safety-critical decisions.
  • Compatibility submissions are evidence for review, not an automatic guarantee that a bike or feature is supported.
Important: Applications are not yet live until the private n8n webhook is configured and tested. The opt-in link will be emailed only to approved testers; it will not be exposed on this public page.
