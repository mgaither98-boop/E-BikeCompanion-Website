Privacy policy

How E-Bike Companion handles information when you use the Android app or apply to become a tester.

Effective date: 24 October 2026

In brief: E-Bike Companion is designed for offline-first, local use. Core features do not require an account. Ride records, app settings and imported maps are stored on your device. Location and Bluetooth are used for features you choose to use. Information leaves your device if you choose to share or submit it, and online map tiles require network requests to the map provider. The separate website tester form collects the details you submit so the project can review your application.

1. Who is responsible?

E-Bike Companion is an independent Android application maintained by its project lead, Michael Gaither. For this policy, the project lead is the contact for privacy requests concerning the app and tester applications. Contact privacy@ebike.magservers.org with questions or requests. General support is available at ebike.magservers.org/support.

E-Bike Companion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Fiido or other bicycle, motor or component manufacturers.

2. Information the Android app uses

  • Location and ride information: If you grant location permission and use GPS features, the app can process location, timestamps, speed and altitude to show your position, record routes and calculate ride statistics. Saved rides can contain a sequence of GPS coordinates and timestamps, which may reveal where and when you travelled.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices: Bluetooth permissions let the app discover and connect to supported bikes or sensors and read information those devices expose. Device names, identifiers available through Bluetooth, connection state, controller telemetry and diagnostic events may be processed on your device.
  • Ride history and settings: Saved ride records, ride samples, preferences, profiles and a custom saved-bike display name are stored in the app's private local storage. The custom display name is a local label; it does not change the bike's Bluetooth identity.
  • Offline maps and routes: Imported raster MBTiles files and imported GPX routes are stored locally for map viewing and route following. A GPX file can contain route coordinates, timestamps, elevation or other metadata depending on the file. Review files before sharing them.
  • Diagnostics and compatibility reports: If you choose to run a compatibility verification or export diagnostics, the generated files may include app version, bike or sensor information, Bluetooth identifiers or characteristics, telemetry values, timestamps, device details and diagnostic events. Verification reports are designed to omit GPS route coordinates, but no sanitisation should be treated as a substitute for checking the actual export. Review every file before sharing it.

3. Why the app uses this information

The app uses this information to provide the features you request: Bluetooth connection and telemetry, ride recording and analysis, map display, route following, settings, troubleshooting and voluntary compatibility research. The app does not require you to create an E-Bike Companion account to use its core features.

Ride recording is user-controlled. Automatic ride detection in the current beta only works while the app is in the foreground; it does not continue recording in the background or while the phone is locked.

4. Local storage, network access and map providers

Ride records, preferences and imported offline map files are intended to remain in the app's private storage on your device. The app is designed not to upload ride history automatically. Android's backup for the app is disabled in the current Android manifest; this does not remove copies you may create yourself by exporting, sharing or backing up files outside the app.

When you view the online map layer, the app requests tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile service at https://tile.openstreetmap.org/. The tile provider and network infrastructure can receive your IP address, requested tile coordinates and ordinary connection metadata. Tile requests can reveal the map area being viewed. Review the OpenStreetMap tile usage policy and OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Imported MBTiles can be viewed offline without requesting those online tiles, provided the selected map pack contains the area and zoom level you need.

Internet access is also used when you choose to open external links or share/send reports through another app or service. Those services receive information according to your action and their own terms and privacy policies. The project does not intentionally include in-app advertising or an analytics service; this is reviewed as part of release preparation and should not be read as a claim that online map providers or services you choose to use receive no technical data.

5. Sharing and compatibility reports

No ride-history upload is intended to happen automatically. If you choose Android Share, email, save/export, GitHub or another external destination, the files and information you select are passed to that destination. Once sent, the recipient or service may retain, copy or further process them under its own policies. Check the recipient and file contents first, especially GPS data, device identifiers, serial numbers, logs and timestamps.

Compatibility reports are intended to support maintainer review. Evidence may be used to investigate telemetry decoding and device compatibility; a report does not automatically make an experimental driver or bike control verified. The app does not embed a GitHub access token or your email credentials for report submission.

6. Website tester applications

If you submit the tester form at ebike.magservers.org/testers, the form collects the details you enter: email address, optional preferred name or handle, Android device and version, optional bike model, selected testing interests, optional notes and your confirmation that you agree to the stated use. Please do not include GPS routes, serial numbers, passwords, health information or other sensitive details.

The website sends the application to a server-side Cloudflare Pages Function, which creates an issue in the private E-Bike Companion app repository using a server-side GitHub credential. The credential is not intended to be exposed to the browser. Application details are intended to be accessible only to people with access to that private repository and are not intended to be posted to the public community repository. Cloudflare and GitHub process the request as service providers and may process technical information such as IP address, request metadata and security logs under their own policies.

Applications are reviewed manually. The email address is used to contact you about your application and, if accepted, to send testing instructions. Providing an application is voluntary and does not guarantee acceptance. Applications should be retained only while needed to manage recruitment and testing; you may request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@ebike.magservers.org. The project lead should delete the private issue and any other copies under the project's control when they are no longer needed or when a valid deletion request is accepted, subject to any legal obligation to retain information. Copies may remain temporarily in service backups or logs controlled by the providers.

7. Legal basis and your privacy rights

Where data protection law applies, the app processes local information to provide the feature you choose to use. For tester applications, the project uses the details you submit to take steps at your request to assess and administer your application; your explicit confirmation is also used to document that you agreed to be contacted for this purpose. You can withdraw that permission for future contact and request deletion by emailing the privacy address above.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in Ireland or the European Union, you can contact the Irish Data Protection Commission through dataprotection.ie. These rights may be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

8. Retention and deletion

Local rides, maps, routes and settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, uninstall the app or otherwise remove the files. Deleting an item in the app does not delete a copy already exported to Downloads, shared with another app, attached to email or submitted to an external service. Those copies must be deleted separately, where possible.

Tester applications are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to review applications and administer the testing programme, unless a longer period is required by law. Email providers, GitHub and Cloudflare may retain technical records or backups under their own retention practices. Contact the privacy address to request deletion of an application held by the project.

9. Security

The app is designed to keep core records in private app storage and make external sharing an intentional user action. Tester applications are submitted server-side to a private repository; repository access and the server-side credential must be restricted to the people and permissions needed to manage applications. No device, storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep Android updated, use a secure screen lock and review exported files before sharing them.

10. Children

E-Bike Companion is a utility for riders and is not designed to target children or knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of location, Bluetooth and sharing features. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the tester form, contact the privacy address to request its review and deletion.

11. Your choices and permissions

You can deny or revoke location and Bluetooth permissions in Android settings, although dependent features may stop working. You can choose whether to record rides, import maps, run compatibility research or share diagnostic information. Use the app's available controls to delete saved rides and imported items; use Android settings to clear the app's remaining local data. You may decline or withdraw a tester application by contacting the privacy address.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a material revision is published. Check this page periodically, especially before submitting a tester application or sharing diagnostics.

13. Contact

Privacy requests: privacy@ebike.magservers.org
General support: support@ebike.magservers.org. These addresses are stated to forward to the project's Gmail inbox; contact routing should be checked periodically to ensure messages are received.

Important scope note: This policy describes the intended behaviour of the Android app and the published tester form. The project lead must keep it aligned with the exact Play Store release, dependency and network behaviour, the deployed tester endpoint, service-provider settings and actual retention/deletion practices. It is not legal advice.
