Bike controls

Understand assist modes, lights and controller-dependent controls in E-Bike Companion.

Available controls depend on your bike

The app can only safely describe or operate controls that are implemented and supported for the detected bike/controller. A label or icon alone does not prove the bike accepts that command.

Before using a control

  1. Park in a safe place and keep the bike stationary.
  2. Confirm the connected bike and compatibility status.
  3. Read the control's description and any warning shown in the app.
  4. Change only a setting that is documented as supported for your setup.
  5. Check any available confirmation/read-back and compare with the bike's own display.

Assist modes, lights and throttle

Mode names and the number of available levels can vary by controller. Throttle, cruise-control and lighting features may not be available on every bike. If the app shows more modes than your bike supports, or a control has no effect, stop using that control and submit a compatibility report.

Safety: This guide does not instruct users to bypass speed limits, modify firmware or send unverified commands. Never test experimental controls while moving.
