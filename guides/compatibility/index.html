Bike compatibility

Understand verified, experimental and untested bike capabilities and what evidence is needed.

Bluetooth discovery is not compatibility proof

A bike or display appearing in a scan only shows that a device was discovered. It does not establish that the app can decode telemetry or safely control the bike.

How to read status

  • Verified: a specific capability has reproducible supporting evidence for the documented setup.
  • Experimental: early investigation exists, but behaviour may be incomplete or unconfirmed.
  • Untested / unknown: there is not enough evidence to claim support.

Check the main compatibility directory for the latest reviewed information.

Submit useful evidence

Include app version/build, Android version, bike model, display/controller identifiers if known, the exact capability tested and repeatable steps. Do not share passwords, serial numbers, GPS routes or unrelated personal data.

See contribution options →

Important: Compatibility may differ between controller revisions and firmware versions, even for bikes sold under the same model name.
