Troubleshooting & FAQs

Start with the symptom, try one change at a time and record what happened.

Bluetooth device not found

  1. Make sure the bike is on and the phone is nearby.
  2. Check Bluetooth and app permissions in Android settings.
  3. Disconnect other apps that may be using the same device.
  4. Retry discovery while stationary. If it remains unavailable, check compatibility and report your setup.

Connected, but some values are blank

A connection can succeed even when a driver cannot interpret every data field. Compare with the bike display, check the compatibility page and report the specific missing value. Do not treat an unknown value as zero.

Offline map shows no detail

Confirm that the MBTiles file covers the area and zoom level. Test while disconnected from the internet; if detail appears only online, the necessary tiles may not be stored locally. See the offline maps guide.

Ride recording or GPS seems inaccurate

Check Android location permission and device location settings. Battery optimisation may affect background recording on some phones. Test outdoors with a clear view of the sky and compare results over a known route.

App crashes or stops responding

  1. Note the exact action immediately before the problem.
  2. Record the app version/build, phone model and Android version.
  3. Reopen the app and see whether the issue repeats.
  4. Use the support page to submit a concise reproduction report. Review diagnostics first and remove personal data.

How do I know if my bike is supported?

Check the compatibility page. Bluetooth discovery alone is not proof of compatibility. Look for the specific bike/controller, capability and evidence status; experimental results should remain clearly distinguished from verified behaviour.

What information should I include in a report?

  • App version and build number
  • Phone model and Android version
  • Bike model, display and controller if known
  • What you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce
  • Relevant error text, with serial numbers, GPS tracks and other private data removed
