USER GUIDE / DIAGNOSTICS

Diagnostics & logs

Review diagnostic information and share reports with care.

When a diagnostic report helps

Logs can help explain connection problems, missing telemetry or reproducible errors. Capture only what is relevant to the issue.

Before sharing

  1. Review the report contents and remove GPS tracks, personal identifiers, serial numbers and unrelated data.
  2. Include the app version/build, Android version, bike model and steps to reproduce.
  3. Describe expected behaviour and actual behaviour separately.
  4. Use the official support or contribution route.

Captures and logs may be incomplete

A missing field does not prove that the bike lacks a capability. A captured packet does not automatically explain its meaning. Do not publish raw data that may expose private information or enable unsafe assumptions.

Privacy first: Share the minimum evidence needed. Do not upload secrets, access tokens or full location histories.
