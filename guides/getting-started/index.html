Getting started

Prepare your phone and learn the basics before connecting to your e-bike.

Before you begin

  • Install E-Bike Companion from the official release or testing channel you have been given.
  • Check the app version and Android version if you need support.
  • Charge your phone and keep it close to the bike during setup.
  • Park safely and keep the bike stationary while testing Bluetooth or controller-related features.

First connection

  1. Turn on the bike using its normal controls.
  2. Enable Bluetooth in Android settings.
  3. Open E-Bike Companion and follow any Android permission prompts it displays. Permission names vary by Android version.
  4. Open the app's bike/device connection area and start discovery if that option is available in your build.
  5. Select your device only when its name or identifier matches the expected bike or component.
  6. Wait for the app to show connection or data status. A device appearing in a scan does not prove that every feature is supported.

If the bike does not appear

  1. Confirm the bike is powered on and the phone is nearby.
  2. Check Bluetooth and the permissions requested by Android.
  3. Close the manufacturer's app or other tool if it may already hold the device's Bluetooth connection.
  4. Stop and restart discovery, then try again.
  5. If it still fails, record the app version, phone/Android version, bike model and what you tried before contacting support.

Some bikes and displays use proprietary protocols or permit only one active connection. Not every Bluetooth device is compatible.

Safety: Never troubleshoot while riding. Do not pair with an unknown device or send experimental commands to your bike.
