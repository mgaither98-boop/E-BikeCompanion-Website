Rides & GPS

Learn about ride recording, location permissions, speed and distance data.

Before recording

  1. Review the location permissions requested by the app in Android settings.
  2. Enable device location if the feature requires it.
  3. Start recording only when safely parked and ready to ride.
  4. Confirm the recording state shown by your installed app.
  5. After the ride, review the saved data and stop recording when finished.

The exact buttons and background behaviour can vary by release and Android version.

Speed, distance and odometer

Values may come from GPS, a bike controller or another supported source. These sources can differ. Treat unavailable values as unknown, and do not use app readings as the sole basis for a safety-critical decision.

If GPS or background recording stops

  • Check Android location permissions and location settings.
  • Test outdoors with a clearer view of the sky.
  • Review battery optimisation settings if recording stops when the app is backgrounded.
  • Record the phone model, Android version, app build and steps to reproduce.
Privacy: Ride records and GPS tracks can reveal personal routines and locations. Review exports before sharing and remove private data that is not needed for troubleshooting.
