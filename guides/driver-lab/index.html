Driver Lab

Learn about experimental Bluetooth discovery and read-only research in E-Bike Companion.

What Driver Lab is for

Driver Lab helps inspect Bluetooth Low Energy discovery and available diagnostic information during careful, stationary research. Results may be incomplete, vendor-specific or not yet understood.

Use it safely

  1. Park the bike and make sure it cannot move unexpectedly.
  2. Connect only to the device you intend to investigate.
  3. Review the displayed status and record what was actually observed.
  4. Leave unknown values unknown; do not guess at command meanings.
  5. Share a sanitised report if you want to help improve compatibility.

Read-only means read-only

Experimental discovery and diagnostic tools should not be treated as permission to write controller registers, alter firmware or issue unverified control commands. Use normal manufacturer controls for ordinary bike operation.

Not for use while riding. Do not use research tools to change speed limits or bypass manufacturer, firmware or legal restrictions.
