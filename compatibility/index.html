Verification criteria

Before publishing a compatibility claim, record the exact bike/model year, display, controller and firmware when known; app version; test method; observations; repeatability; and limitations. Separate read-only telemetry observations from any control-related capability. Do not publish personal identifiers, serial numbers or GPS routes.
