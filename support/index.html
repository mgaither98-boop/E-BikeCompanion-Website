Support & help

Find setup guidance, troubleshoot common issues and share compatibility feedback safely.

Start here

For the fastest help, include your app version, Android version and the steps that led to the problem. Never post passwords, account tokens or unredacted personal data.

⌁

Bluetooth connection

Turn on Bluetooth, grant the permissions Android requests, keep the bike powered on and nearby, and close the manufacturer's app if it is already connected. Some bikes allow only one active connection.

⌖

GPS & ride recording

Allow location access for ride features, check that device location is enabled, and confirm Android battery restrictions are not stopping background activity. GPS accuracy depends on device and surroundings.

▦

Offline maps

Use a supported raster MBTiles file, make sure there is enough free storage, and allow the import to finish. Online map tiles need an internet connection; offline maps must be imported in advance.

◈

Bike compatibility

Compatibility depends on bike model, display, controller and firmware. Experimental drivers are read-only and may expose incomplete telemetry. Do not assume that one model's support applies to another.
